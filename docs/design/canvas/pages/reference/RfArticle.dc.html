Steps, choices, branches
How steps, choices and branches work
A quest is a list of steps. The player sees one step at a time. Each step has a journal line and ends when its condition is met.
Choices
A dialogue line can offer up to 3 choices. Each choice goes to a step, so the quest can branch. The game shows at most 3 choices. Chain another line for more.
Branches
Two choices that go to different steps make a branch. Branches can meet again at a later step.
